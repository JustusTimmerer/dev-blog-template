A quick bite is a short post that takes less than five minutes to read. It has one idea and no series.

A glossary term can point to a bite. Then the reader sees the title and the summary when they hover over the term, and a click opens the bite in a new tab. Set `bite: example-bite` in the front matter of the glossary file.
